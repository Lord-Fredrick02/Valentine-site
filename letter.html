For My Love ❤️

My Love,

I've been thinking about you, and I realized I don't tell you nearly enough what you mean to me. So here it is, in no particular order, just everything I want you to know.

You make life feel sharper and softer at the same time. Sharp, because you challenge me to be better, more patient, more thoughtful, more present. Soft, because even in the hardest moments, your presence is a reminder that not everything has to be heavy, that laughter, comfort, and peace can exist in the same room.

I love the way your mind works, how you notice the little things I miss, how you see patterns in moments most people would overlook. You make me feel understood in ways I didn't even realize I needed, and that's rare. I've met people, shared time with people, but you…. You see me, and you choose to stay.

I love how our silences feel alive, how conversations with you are both deep and effortlessly light. I love the way you carry yourself, the way you care without demanding, the way you laugh like it's a secret only I get to hear.

I love the way we grow together, and the way you hold space for my growth, even when it's messy or slow. I love that you make me want to leave a better footprint in the world, not because someone told me to, but because I want you to see the best of me every day.

Most of all, I love us. Our moments, our jokes, the plans we whisper about when no one's listening, the way you make ordinary days feel extraordinary. You are my peace, my spark, my anchor, my excitement, my all in one person.

So today, on Valentine's Day, I don't just want to say "I love you." I want to say: I see you, I value you, I respect you, and I am endlessly grateful that in a world full of chances and choices, you chose me. And I choose you, every single day, without hesitation.

Thank you for being my person, my safe space, my adventure, my love. I hope I can be all of that for you too.

Forever yours,
Fredrick
